PHASE 3 — EXTENSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Subgroup fairness   : largest gap 0.242 C-index (STAD/age_group, CoxRidge:
                      >65 vs <=50); mean gap across strata 0.065. Age is the
                      dominant axis (mean gap 0.099-0.100), then subtype
                      (0.063-0.068), sex (0.053-0.055), stage (0.026-0.036).
Lightweight equity  : shrinking the VAE changes C-index by -0.003 on low-event cohorts vs +0.005 on high-event cohorts
Robustness          : missing=50%: CoxRidge 0.604 (from 0.632), VAECox 0.630 (from 0.631); noise=sigma=2.0: CoxRidge 0.589 (from 0.632), VAECox 0.628 (from 0.631)
Risk stratification : 5/10 cohorts show significant high/low risk separation (log-rank p<0.05)
CPU accessibility   : k=1000 genes, 1.08M-param VAE trains on CPU in 11.4s; C-index 0.626
Interpretability    : permutation importance over 3 cohorts
                      x 2 models

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEVIATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - HP search reduced vs paper's 18-combo 5-fold CV (time). Documented.
  - Extensions use fixed HP ({'CoxLasso': (0.0001, 0.0), 'CoxRidge': (0.0001, 0.001), 'Coxnnet': (0.001, 1e-05), 'CoxMLP': (0.001, 1e-05), 'VAECox': (0.001, 1e-05)}) rather than the per-cancer search,
    so extension C-indices are not directly comparable to the Phase 2 table.
  - VAE minibatched on GPU (paper full-batch); numerically equivalent.
  - Lightweight sweep pretrains every config for 100 epochs (not
    500) so configs are compared at an equal compute budget.
  - Robustness (6j.2) uses 5 seeds; subgroup fairness (6h) uses all 10.
  - Subgroup gaps exclude TCGA free-text histology placeholders ("Other,
    specify" / "Mixed Histology (please specify)"): 4 strata dropped, listed in
    subgroup_excluded_strata.csv. They are not clinical categories, so a gap
    between two of them measures nothing. Recomputing without them moves the
    largest gap from 0.475 to 0.242 and the subtype mean from 0.173 to 0.063
    (CoxRidge) / 0.116 to 0.068 (VAECox).
  - GenoTEX HiSeqV2_PANCAN gene set (20530) may differ slightly from
    paper's 20,502 (pan-cancer-normalised vs per-cohort). Documented.
  - Subgroup strata below 10 patients or 3 events are dropped as
    unestimable rather than reported.
